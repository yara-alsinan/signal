This is Signal, Yara Alsinan's individual term project: a research assistant for understanding changes in company financial results.

00:00:10–00:00:40 | Pitch: Company research takes repeated work
Understanding a company means more than looking at its stock price. People need to compare financial results, find the right reporting periods, and check the evidence behind an explanation. Signal aims to reduce that repeated work. It builds on Stock Sense by investigating the company behind the stock. The current prototype focuses on NVIDIA. Whether people find this useful enough to return remains something to test.
